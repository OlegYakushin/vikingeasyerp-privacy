VikingEasyERP Mobile

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 16, 2026

VikingEasyERP Mobile is an enterprise employee application. Access is available only to authorized users whose accounts are issued by an organization using VikingEasyERP.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use VikingEasyERP Mobile (the “App”). The organization that issued your account (“Customer Organization”) determines which workforce features are enabled and ordinarily controls the employment and attendance information processed through the App. VikingEasyERP provides the application and related platform services on behalf of the Customer Organization.

Information we process

  • Account and profile information: name, employee number, email address, organization, role, and account identifiers.
  • Authentication and security information: sign-in credentials in protected form, session tokens, trusted-device status, request identifiers, and security signals used to protect accounts and the service.
  • Attendance information: punch-in, presence, and punch-out actions, dates, times, status, assigned workplace, and attendance history.
  • Precise location: location captured when an attendance action requires verification that you are at an authorized workplace.
  • Face and camera information: camera images and face-related verification data used for enrollment, liveness checks, and identity verification. The App does not obtain your Apple Face ID template.
  • Employee-service content: requests, approvals, comments, dates, status, and files or images you choose to submit through enabled HR workflows.
  • Device and notification information: device and installation identifiers, push-notification token, device model, operating-system and App versions, language, locale, and time zone.
  • Technical activity: feature interactions and limited diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide and protect the App’s business functions, including authentication, employee profile display, workplace attendance, face enrollment and verification, HR requests and approvals, notifications, customer support, fraud and misuse prevention, service reliability, and compliance with the Customer Organization’s policies and applicable law.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested when face enrollment or verification is used.
  • Location: requested while the App is in use when workplace attendance verification is required.
  • Notifications: optional permission used for work-related updates.

You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be available to authorized personnel of the Customer Organization and to service providers that support hosting, security, and notification delivery, including Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. These providers may process information only to deliver their services and must protect it appropriately. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the Customer Organization, or the service.

We do not sell personal information. The App does not use personal information for third-party advertising and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Storage, security, and retention

Information is transmitted using encrypted network connections. Authentication material stored on the device uses protected system storage. Access to business data is restricted to authorized accounts and roles.

Records are retained according to the Customer Organization’s instructions, operational needs, and applicable employment, accounting, security, and legal requirements. Retention periods may therefore vary by organization and record type. Data is deleted, de-identified, or restricted when it is no longer required, subject to legal retention obligations.

Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or raise another privacy question, by contacting the Customer Organization that issued your account. Because employment and attendance records are controlled by that organization, it is normally the fastest point of contact. You may also use the support channel provided by your organization or visit vikingerp.com.

Signing out removes the active App session. Deleting the App from a device does not automatically delete records held by the Customer Organization. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or legitimate business obligations.

International processing

Depending on the Customer Organization and its service configuration, information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Appropriate contractual, organizational, and technical safeguards are used where required.

Children

The App is intended for authorized workforce users and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version and its effective date will remain available at this URL.

Contact

For privacy requests, contact the Customer Organization that issued your VikingEasyERP account. For platform information and support, visit https://vikingerp.com.
